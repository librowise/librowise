Self-checkout kiosks

Provision touch-screen stations where patrons borrow and renew on their own. Each kiosk has its own secret token and works for one branch.
Open kiosk screen

Devices

How it works

  1. Create a kiosk for the branch where the station stands. Copy its token — it is shown only once.
  2. On the station, open /kiosk and paste the token, or use Set up this device when you are working on the station itself (your staff session is signed out automatically).
  3. Patrons sign in with their card number and password. Normal loan rules apply with no overrides, and items reserved for other readers cannot be borrowed.
  4. Sessions end after a short period of inactivity, with a countdown. Lost or stolen station? Deactivate it or rotate its token.
